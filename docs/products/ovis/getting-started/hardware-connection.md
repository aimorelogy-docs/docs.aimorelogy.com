# Assemble and Connect OVIS

OVIS consists of a camera board and an OVIS Core board. Assemble the two boards in the correct orientation before connecting USB or UART0.

:::caution

Disconnect USB and UART before assembling or separating the boards.

:::

## Assemble the boards

1. Locate the white orientation mark at the upper-left corner of each board.
2. Hold the boards so the white corners point in the same direction, then align the board-to-board connectors.
3. Keep the boards parallel and press them together evenly until the connectors are fully seated.

![Align the white corner marks and assemble the OVIS camera board with the OVIS Core board](/img/products/ovis/hardware-connection/01-align-and-assemble.png)

## Identify the interfaces

The numbered areas in the image show the interfaces used for connection and firmware flashing:

1. **USB** interface.
2. **UART0** serial interface.

![USB and UART0 interface locations on the OVIS Core board](/img/products/ovis/hardware-connection/02-usb-uart0-connectors.png)
